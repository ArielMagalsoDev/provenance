# Equipment

## Printing

The shared printer/scanner is available on every floor. Black-and-white pages are free
up to each tier's monthly allowance (see Membership Limits) and $0.10 per page beyond
that allowance. Color pages are $0.35 per page regardless of tier and are never included
in the free allowance. Print jobs are released by tapping a member's keycard at the
printer.

## Meeting room AV equipment

Conference and Boardroom rooms include a wall-mounted display with HDMI and USB-C
input, a conferencing speakerphone, and built-in video conferencing camera and
microphone compatible with common video call platforms. Huddle rooms include a smaller
wall-mounted display with HDMI/USB-C input but no dedicated conferencing camera. Members
should report any AV issue to the front desk during staffed hours; there is no
after-hours AV support.

## Loaner equipment

The front desk keeps a small pool of loaner equipment available on a first-come,
first-served basis at no charge: HDMI and USB-C adapters, phone chargers (USB-C,
Lightning, and micro-USB), and a limited number of loaner laptop chargers for common
brands. Loaner items must be returned to the front desk the same day they are borrowed.

## Standing desks and desk accessories

Every desk in the open lounge and in shared pods is a sit-stand adjustable desk.
Dedicated Desk and Private Office members may request a monitor arm, footrest, or desk
lamp from the front desk at no additional charge, subject to availability. External
monitors are not provided by Meridian Nine and are not available as loaner equipment —
members who want a second monitor at their desk should bring their own.

## Kitchen equipment

Each floor's community kitchen includes a drip coffee machine, an espresso machine
(Dedicated Desk and Private Office floors only), a filtered water tap, a microwave, and
a refrigerator with labeled shelf space available to any member on a first-come basis.
Members should label perishable items with their name and date; unlabeled items are
discarded weekly during kitchen cleaning.

## Equipment for outside events

Outside organizations booking the Boardroom for an event (see Guest Policy) may request
additional AV equipment such as a projector or extra microphones through the front desk
at an additional rate quoted at booking time; availability is not guaranteed and should
be confirmed at least 5 business days before the event.
