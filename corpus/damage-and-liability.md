# Damage & Liability

## Member responsibility for personal property

Members are responsible for their own personal belongings, laptops, and business
equipment at all times. Meridian Nine does not accept responsibility for personal
property that is lost, stolen, or damaged while on the premises, whether left at an
assigned desk, in an office, or in a shared area.

## Damage to Meridian Nine property

A member who damages Meridian Nine furniture, equipment, walls, or fixtures — whether
by accident or otherwise — is responsible for the cost of repair or replacement at fair
market value. Damage should be reported to the front desk as soon as it happens; damage
discovered later that can reasonably be traced to a specific member's use (for example,
a booked room found damaged immediately after that booking) will be billed to that
member's account.

## Found property

Items found in shared areas are held at the front desk for 30 days and logged in the
lost-and-found register. Unclaimed items are donated or discarded after 30 days.
Meridian Nine is not liable for the condition of found items while they are being held.

## Premises insurance

Meridian Nine Coworking LLC carries commercial general liability insurance covering the
physical premises and common areas, consistent with standard commercial landlord
practice. As noted in the Guest Policy, outside organizations booking event space must
separately provide their own certificate of insurance naming Meridian Nine as
additionally insured for the duration of their event.

## Injury on premises

A member or guest who is injured on the premises should report the incident to the
front desk immediately so an incident report can be filed. Members and guests use
common areas, meeting rooms, and all facilities at their own risk; Meridian Nine's
premises insurance covers the building and common areas as noted above, but members
and guests are expected to exercise reasonable care in their own use of the space.

## Community conduct and space damage from misuse

Repeated damage or a pattern of misuse of shared equipment or furniture may result in a
conversation with Meridian Nine staff and, in serious or repeated cases, suspension of
membership in addition to any repair costs billed to the member's account.
