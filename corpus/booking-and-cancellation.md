# Booking & Cancellation

## How to book a meeting room

Meeting rooms are booked through the member portal or the Meridian Nine mobile app.
Rooms can be booked up to 30 days in advance and in 30-minute increments, with a 30-
minute minimum booking length. There are three room sizes: Huddle (seats 2-4, standard
rate $12/hour), Conference (seats 6-10, standard rate $22/hour), and Boardroom (seats up
to 16, standard rate $38/hour, includes video conferencing equipment). Tier-included
credit (see Pricing) is applied automatically to any room type before the standard rate
is charged for remaining time.

## Cancelling a booking

A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 4 hours before its start time. Credit or
payment used for that booking is fully restored. Cancellations made less than 4 hours
before the start time forfeit the credit or payment for that booking — there is no
partial refund for a late cancellation.

## No-shows

A booking with no check-in within 15 minutes of its start time is automatically released
and marked as a no-show. No-shows forfeit the credit or payment used, same as a late
cancellation. A member with 3 no-shows in a rolling 30-day period loses the ability to
book more than 24 hours in advance for the following 30 days.

## Recurring bookings

Members can set up a recurring weekly booking for the same room and time slot, subject
to availability. A recurring series can be cancelled as a whole or edited one occurrence
at a time from the portal. Each occurrence in a recurring series follows the same 4-hour
cancellation window as a one-off booking.

## Same-day and walk-up bookings

Rooms with no reservation on the calendar can be booked same-day, including on a
walk-up basis by checking room availability on the lobby display screen. Walk-up
bookings still deduct from the member's credit balance or charge the standard rate the
same as an advance booking.

## Day Pass booking restrictions

As noted in Pricing, Day Pass holders cannot reserve a room more than 2 hours in
advance and cannot use tier credit (they have none) — Day Pass room use is always billed
at the standard hourly rate shown above.

## Building-wide closures

If Meridian Nine closes a room or the building for maintenance or a scheduled closure
(see Hours & Access for holiday closures), any existing bookings in the affected window
are cancelled automatically and any credit or payment used is fully restored — this is
not treated as a member cancellation and does not count toward the no-show limit.
