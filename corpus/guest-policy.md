# Guest Policy

## Bringing a guest

All guests must be signed in by their host member at the front desk during standard
building hours and must wear a visitor badge for the duration of their visit. A guest
cannot enter using a member's keycard and cannot be signed in remotely — the host member
must be on-site to sign a guest in.

## Guest visit allowances

Flex members may bring up to 4 guest visits per month at no charge. Dedicated Desk
members get 8 guest visits per month at no charge. Private Office members have unlimited
guest visits. A "visit" is counted per guest per day regardless of how long they stay.
Guest visits beyond a tier's monthly allowance are billed at $10 per guest per visit.

## What guests can access

A signed-in guest may sit with their host member in the open lounge and use guest wifi
(a separate network from member wifi, issued at the front desk per visit). Guests cannot
independently book meeting rooms, use the printer, or access any keycard-restricted
area; a guest using a meeting room must be accompanied by their host member, and the
booking is made under the host's account and draws from the host's credit.

## Guests at private events

A member hosting a larger gathering (more than 6 guests at once) in a booked meeting
room should notify the front desk in advance so extra badges can be prepared. Groups
above 15 people are treated as an outside event booking rather than a standard guest
visit — see the note on outside event bookings below.

## Outside event bookings

Meridian Nine's Boardroom and Conference rooms can be booked by outside organizations
for private events outside of a member's guest allowance, subject to availability and a
separate event rate quoted by the front desk. Any outside group booking an event space
must provide a certificate of insurance naming Meridian Nine Coworking LLC as
additionally insured before the event date, regardless of event size.

## Guest conduct

Guests are expected to follow the same community guidelines as members, including quiet
hours in focus areas and normal building courtesy. A host member is responsible for
their guest's conduct while on premises.
