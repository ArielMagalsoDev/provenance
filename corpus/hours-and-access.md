# Hours & Access

## Standard building hours

Meridian Nine's standard hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and
Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The building is closed to members on Sundays except for
Private Office members, who have 24/7 access every day of the week regardless of the
standard schedule.

## 24/7 access

Dedicated Desk and Private Office members get 24/7 keycard access every day, including
Sundays and holidays. Flex and Day Pass members are limited to standard building hours
listed above and cannot enter outside those windows even with a valid keycard.

## Keycard access

Every membership above Day Pass includes a personal keycard, issued at the front desk
on the member's first visit after signup. A lost keycard should be reported immediately
through the member portal so it can be deactivated; a replacement keycard costs $15.
Keycards are personal and should not be shared — access is logged per card, and repeated
shared use may result in the card being deactivated pending a conversation with the
member.

## Holiday closures

Meridian Nine is closed to Flex, Dedicated Desk (for staffed services, though keycard
access remains available), and Day Pass members on New Year's Day, Independence Day,
Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Front desk staff and community programming are not
available on these days even though the building itself remains accessible to
badge-holding members. The front desk posts the full holiday schedule for the coming
year each December.

## Building entrance and location

Meridian Nine's main entrance is on Fifth Street, one block from the Fernbridge Transit
Center, which serves the Blue and Green light rail lines and several bus routes. The
Fifth Street entrance has street-level access with no stairs from the sidewalk to the
lobby. A secondary entrance off the rear alley is available for deliveries and is not a
member entry point during standard hours.

## Wifi and technology

All members receive a private, password-protected wifi network with a unique login
issued at signup, separate from the guest wifi network described in the Guest Policy.
Wifi speeds are not tiered by membership level — every member gets the same connection.

## Front desk staffing

The front desk is staffed during standard building hours listed above. Outside those
hours, the space operates unstaffed for members with 24/7 access; there is no on-site
staff to assist with issues overnight or on Sundays, though the after-hours support line
posted at the front desk can be called for building emergencies.
