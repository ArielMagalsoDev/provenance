# Refunds

## Membership refunds

Monthly memberships (Day Pass, Flex, Dedicated Desk) are not refundable once a billing
cycle has started. A member who cancels mid-cycle keeps access through the end of the
cycle they already paid for, per the cancellation terms in Pricing, but does not receive
a prorated refund for the unused portion of that cycle.

## Annual Private Office refunds

Annual Private Office contracts (see Pricing) may be cancelled early with 60 days'
written notice. Cancelling an annual contract early forfeits the 10% annual discount
retroactively — the member is charged the difference between the discounted annual rate
already paid and the standard month-to-month rate for the months already used, then
refunded any remaining balance from the original annual payment.

## Day Pass refunds

A Day Pass is refundable in full if cancelled at least 2 hours before the visit date via
the member portal. A Day Pass is non-refundable once that window has passed or once the
member has checked in at the front desk.

## Meeting room credit and payment refunds

Refunds for meeting room bookings follow the cancellation terms in Booking &
Cancellation: a booking cancelled at least 4 hours ahead is refunded in full (credit
restored or payment reversed); a late cancellation or no-show is not refunded.

## How refunds are issued

Any approved refund is issued to the original payment method on file. Card refunds
typically post within 5-10 business days depending on the card issuer. Meridian Nine
does not issue cash or check refunds under any circumstance.

## Billing disputes

A member who believes they were charged in error has 30 days from the charge date to
raise a dispute through the member portal's billing section or by emailing
billing@meridiannine.example. Disputes raised after 30 days are reviewed at Meridian
Nine's discretion and are not guaranteed a refund.
