# Membership Limits

## Occupancy and desk limits

Each Flex or Dedicated Desk membership covers one named individual. A membership is not
transferable between people day to day — the person using the space should be the
member on file, not a coworker or friend using their card. Members who want a colleague
to have regular access should get that colleague their own membership or add them as a
guest under the Guest Policy.

## Meeting room credit limits

Meeting room credit does not roll over between months. Unused hours expire at the end
of each billing cycle. Flex members get 8 hours/month, Dedicated Desk members get 16
hours/month, and Private Office members have unlimited credit. Credit is deducted in
30-minute increments, rounded up.

## Printing limits

Every membership tier except Day Pass includes 100 free black-and-white pages per
month. Pages beyond that allowance, and all color pages, are billed per the rates in the
Equipment policy. Unused page allowance does not roll over.

## Storage limits

Dedicated Desk members get one lockable under-desk drawer, roughly 12 inches deep.
Private Office members can store items freely within their office. Flex and Day Pass
members have no on-site storage and should not leave personal items in the open lounge
overnight — Meridian Nine is not responsible for items left in open, shared areas (see
Damage & Liability).

## Simultaneous bookings

A single membership can hold at most 2 confirmed meeting room bookings at any given
time. A third booking cannot be placed until one of the first two either occurs or is
cancelled. This limit exists per member, not per Team Account — each teammate on a
pooled Team Account can independently hold 2 bookings even though they share one credit
balance.

## Guest limits

See the Guest Policy document for the number of guest visits included per tier per
month.
