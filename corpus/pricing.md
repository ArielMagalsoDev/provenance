# Pricing & Membership Tiers

Meridian Nine Coworking offers four membership tiers. All prices are in USD and billed
in advance. There is no long-term contract on any tier — every plan is cancel-anytime
unless otherwise noted.

## Day Pass

A Day Pass costs $28 and grants access to the open work lounge on the 2nd floor from
8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on the day of purchase. Day Pass holders get one drip coffee or tea
from the community kitchen, standard wifi, and access to the shared printer at the
per-page rate described in the Equipment policy. Day Pass holders cannot book meeting
rooms in advance — meeting rooms are available to Day Pass holders only on a
walk-up, space-available basis.

## Flex Membership

Flex Membership costs $189 per month. It includes unlimited access to the open work
lounge on the 2nd and 3rd floors during standard building hours, 8 hours of meeting room
credit per month (see Booking & Cancellation for how credits work), unlimited coffee and
tea, and priority printer queue. Flex members do not get an assigned desk — seating is
first-come, first-served among Flex and Day Pass members.

Flex Membership is billed monthly on the date of signup. A member who signs up on the
14th of the month is billed on the 14th of every subsequent month.

## Dedicated Desk

Dedicated Desk costs $429 per month. It includes everything in Flex Membership plus a
permanently assigned desk in a shared pod (4-6 desks per pod), a lockable under-desk
storage drawer, 16 hours of meeting room credit per month, and 24/7 building access via
keycard (see Hours & Access for details on after-hours entry). Dedicated Desk members
may leave a second monitor, keyboard, and personal items at their desk between visits.

## Private Office

Private Office pricing starts at $1,150 per month for a 1-2 person office and scales up
by office size; the largest standard private office (accommodates up to 6 people) is
$3,400 per month. Exact pricing for a specific office depends on square footage and is
quoted at tour time. Private Office includes everything in Dedicated Desk, unlimited
meeting room credit, a lockable door, and the member's business name listed on the
building directory in the lobby.

Private Office is the only tier available on an annual contract with a 10% discount;
month-to-month Private Office is also available at the standard rate.

## What is not included in any tier

No membership tier includes printing beyond the free monthly page allowance described
in the Equipment policy — additional pages are billed per-page. Meeting room credit
beyond a tier's monthly allowance is billed at the standard hourly rate described in
Booking & Cancellation.

## Team Accounts

Two or more Flex or Dedicated Desk memberships under the same company name can be
linked into a Team Account at signup or at any later renewal date. Linking memberships
into a Team Account pools each member's meeting room credit into a single shared balance
for the team, and lets any teammate book a room against that pooled balance. Team
Accounts do not change the per-seat price — each seat is still billed individually at
its tier's monthly rate, and the team's card on file is charged once per seat per
billing cycle.

## Switching or cancelling a tier

Members can upgrade tiers at any time; the new rate applies starting the next billing
cycle. Downgrading follows the same rule — a downgrade submitted mid-cycle takes effect
at the next renewal, not immediately, and the member keeps their current tier's access
for the remainder of the paid cycle. To cancel entirely, a member gives notice through
the member portal at least 5 business days before their next billing date; membership
access continues through the end of the already-paid cycle.
